# KMP Audit Service

I am Rudra Dave, Senior Android Engineer with 6 years of Kotlin Multiplatform experience. I have shipped KMP apps to Google Play (Sotto, VellumLedger, SwiggyMind). I can audit your Android or KMP codebase and give you a written report with findings and recommendations.

## What I audit

| Area | What I check |
|---|---|
| **KMP readiness** | Is your codebase ready for shared business logic across Android and iOS? What would need to change? |
| **Offline-first architecture** | Do you handle network loss gracefully? Room + WorkManager patterns, conflict resolution, retry logic. |
| **Gradle build performance** | Configuration cache, build cache, parallel execution, KAPT vs KSP, JVM heap. Same rules as droidperf but applied to your specific project. |
| **gRPC / Protobuf integration** | Schema evolution safety, backward compatibility patterns, error handling on the client. |
| **General Android architecture** | MVVM, MVI, state management, dependency injection, Compose structure, navigation, testing coverage. |
